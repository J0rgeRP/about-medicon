Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Is Medicon really free?

Yes — 100% free, with no ads or in-app purchases. If you find it useful, you can optionally support its development with a coffee.

Does it need an internet connection or an account?

No. Medicon works fully offline and requires no sign-up: your data is stored only on your device.

Can I manage medication for my family?

Of course. Create unlimited independent profiles and keep each person's medicines, intakes and history separate.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Export your full history to CSV from the old device and import it on the new one. Your data never touches any server.

How do I install the APK safely?

Download it from this site or from Huawei AppGallery, allow installs from your browser when Android asks, and open the file. Updates are announced right here.
